Hello, I'm Maria

Building complete web products, front to back

I'm a Full-Stack Software Engineer and Web Developer with a MSc in Software Engineering (STEM) with Distinction from De Montfort University. My research on benchmarking AI models for automated code generation was published on IEEE Xplore and presented at an international conference at the Universidad de Málaga.

On the front end, I build high-performance, responsive and accessible interfaces for AI, data and cloud enterprise solutions. On the back end, I write server-side logic in PHP and SQL, design and manage databases, build automated data pipelines and booking workflows, and look after the systems that keep live services running, including supplier databases with real-time pricing validation.

I have delivered end-to-end websites, chatbot automation and backend systems with measurable gains in operational efficiency, data accuracy and user experience, and I independently built and maintain a fully functional e-commerce platform with products, carts, secure payments and a database for a live retail brand. I work across HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React.js, PHP, SQL and WordPress, and I deliver on time, every time.
